Particularly, my interests lie in geometric and topological methods applied to and informed by quantum field theory, string theory and holography. I am currently looking to apply to PhD programs for the 2026-2027 cycle. In the above sections you will find some of the work I've done/am currently working on. My most recent interests have shifted towards entanglement entropy but I spend a lot of time thinking about cohomology theories and how they relate to physics at-large!
